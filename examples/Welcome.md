# Welcome to Parchment

A local place for notes, drafts, and ideas.

## Try the views

Press **Control-S** for a split view. Press **Control-R** for rendered-only view or **Control-E** for raw-only view.

### Make it yours

Open **Themes** with **⌘5**. Each of the ten themes includes light and dark palettes.

## Leave a thought

Select this sentence and press **Control-M** to start a comment thread.

Reply in the Comments sidebar. Resolve a thread when the change is complete. The history remains available.
